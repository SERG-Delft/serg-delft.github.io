**Title**: CHAINS: Consistent Hardening and Analysis of Software Supply Chains

**Abstract:**
CHAINS is a research project at KTH about hardening the software supply chain, incl. dependency engineering as well as reproducible, executable and verifiable builds and SBOMs. We primarily look at Maven, NPM, and the software supply chain of crypto. The talk will present our recent results on build and runtime integrity. See <https://chains.proj.kth.se/>

**Bio:** Martin Monperrus is Professor of Software Technology at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden and IEEE fellow. His research lies in the field of software engineering with a current focus on automatic program repair, AI on code and program hardening.

Homepage: <https://www.monperrus.net/martin/>
